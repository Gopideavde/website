FOUNDER'S VISION

𝔖𝔲𝔫𝔦𝔱𝔞 𝔄𝔤𝔯𝔞𝔴𝔞𝔩, 𝔉𝔬𝔲𝔫𝔡𝔢𝔯

“Juhu Beach is a common attraction for Mumbaikars to unwind from their daily hustle and bustle. I hence envisioned flagging off Bayleaf Café to complement and complete their beach experience with a cozy, relaxing café offering diverse, delicious cuisines that appeal to all, while keeping sustainability and health as a top priority. Following the love and response we received, we are delighted to extend this experience further with our new outlet in Goregaon, bringing the same warmth, flavors, and conscious dining philosophy to another part of the city.

We have sourced a majority of the materials for the décor and art pieces ethically and ensured they are recyclable. Our daily operations also steer away from single-use plastics
— be it straws, stirrers, packaging, or even PET bottles.

We at Bayleaf Café look forward to presenting this unique experience not only to Mumbaikars but also to visiting travelers from India and across the world.”

Bayleaf Café specializes in a cooking style that is artistic, contemporary, fusion-based, and extremely flavor-driven, without losing the essence of its roots.

ABOUT OUR BRAND

At Bayleaf, we believe dining is as much about the space as it is about the plate.

Designed with natural textures, warm light, and a sense of openness, our cafés are built to feel inviting yet refined.

Our food draws inspiration from across the world, brought together with a focus on flavour, balance, and detail.

Every visit is meant to feel unhurried — a moment to pause, indulge, and connect.
